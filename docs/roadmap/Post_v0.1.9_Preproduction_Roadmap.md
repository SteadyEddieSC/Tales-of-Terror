# Post-v0.1.9 Preproduction Roadmap — Superseded Historical Record

**Status:** Superseded as the active roadmap after completion of P0.19  
**Original baseline:** `2151696e76147cc9fbe7f56709e585753cb331a3`  
**Successor:** [Post-P0.19 Production-Candidate Roadmap](Post_P0.19_Production_Candidate_Roadmap.md)

This document originally governed the separate P-prefixed preproduction stream that began after playable release v0.1.9. Its foundational direction remains historically important, but its former identification of P0.1 as the current target, its unresolved issue #44 state, and its global local/Codex block are no longer current project status.

## Historical outcome

The planned preproduction work expanded beyond the original outline and completed as P0.1 through P0.19:

- narrative, continuity, dialogue, visual, audio, music, voice, localization, captions, and accessible-narrative direction;
- cross-media traceability and Tale-authoring schemas;
- shared-screen storyboards and interaction-state contracts;
- isolated Drowned Harbor prototype authorization;
- deterministic state/projection fixtures;
- Low Tide, Bellhouse/recovery, controlled-private, and High Water test-only feature proofs;
- aggregate deterministic automation and ordinary-export exclusion.

The historical P0.1–P0.7 inventory remains frozen in `docs/preproduction/preproduction_package_index_v1.json`. Later package history is recorded by the P0.8–P0.19 release summaries and `docs/preproduction/post_prototype_status_v1.json`.

## Preserved principles

The following principles remain active:

- preproduction packages do not silently create production gameplay authority;
- Lantern House remains the sole production Tale until a separate release changes the catalog and provider;
- Drowned Harbor prototype content remains test-only and export-excluded until separately promoted;
- automation is not human evidence;
- issue #39 governs human playtest evidence;
- issue #7 governs final title and host-name commitment;
- generated or externally sourced assets require provenance, licensing, and production-status records;
- one bounded release is active at a time.

## Current routing

Work is no longer globally blocked from local or Codex implementation. Instead, each release requires explicit authorization, an exact protected-main baseline, one bounded issue and branch, declared paths, exclusions, and independent Release Management review.

The active roadmap is now `docs/roadmap/Post_P0.19_Production_Candidate_Roadmap.md`.

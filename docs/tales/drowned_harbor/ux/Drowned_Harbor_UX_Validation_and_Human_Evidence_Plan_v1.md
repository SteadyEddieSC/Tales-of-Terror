# DH-UX-001 — Validation and Human-Evidence Plan v1

## Standing

This document proposes future evidence work. It records no implementation,
validation result, accessibility compliance, television-readability result,
physical-controller result, privacy certification, production readiness, or
shipping authorization.

The registration validator proves only metadata and governance facts:

- exact package and source identities;
- corrected schema closure;
- authority ownership;
- advisory/non-implemented coordinate standing;
- stage order and confirmation categories;
- stable-seat identity requirements;
- privacy exclusions;
- no new legal actions;
- placeholder microcopy;
- conversion readiness remains `not_ready`;
- implementation remains unauthorized;
- binary, candidate, public-distribution, issue #7, issue #39, and PR #32
  boundaries remain closed.

## Future layout checks

After a separately authorized implementation exists, static or capture tooling
should evaluate:

- all critical regions remain within the 960×540 critical safe area;
- the caption reserve intentionally overlays only a governed non-critical board
  zone;
- decision content does not hide required board context;
- stable-seat rail and prompt strip do not overlap;
- every irreversible action has a distinct confirmation state;
- preview states are non-mutating;
- critical icon states have text or a secondary shape channel;
- 1–8 stable seats fit without horizontal scrolling;
- stable-seat identity and order persist through transformations and control
  changes;
- public layouts contain no private fields;
- replay remains presentation-only;
- cleanup remains coordinator-owned.

- all eight stages;

- standard and 135% expanded text;
- reduced-motion settled states;

  reconnecting, game-controlled, takeover-pending, and private-shield states.

No capture exists through this release.

## Issue #39 human evidence

Suggested living-room tasks:

1. Identify stage, Tide, objective, and active seat within a future threshold.
2. Identify each seat in one-, four-, and eight-seat layouts without color.
3. Distinguish open, flooded, damaged, and water-only routes.
4. Preview and cancel without accidental commitment.
5. Confirm an irreversible action and explain when it committed.
6. Understand reconnecting, game control, and takeover pending.
7. Explain the neutral private shield without inferring private content.
8. Read captions and objective text from representative television distance.
9. Use transcript and presentation replay without abandoning a decision.
10. Compare Spooky and Grim and confirm information equivalence.
11. Use reduced motion through High Water and Last Light.
12. Resume after interruption and identify the settled state.

Possible evidence dimensions:

- task success;
- time to locate critical information;
- accidental commits;
- route-state accuracy;
- seat-identification accuracy;
- private-boundary comprehension;
- caption readability;
- motion discomfort;
- controller handoff errors;
- subjective confidence.

Exact thresholds remain an issue #39 decision.

## Stop rules

Stop and return to Release Coordination if work would require:

- a new runtime field;
- a new legal action;
- private disclosure;
- a stable-seat identity change;
- a different route or outcome by presentation profile;
- a source-art or private-surface decision;
- binary repository storage;
- Godot work or Codex;
- accessibility or human-evidence claims without issue #39 authority.

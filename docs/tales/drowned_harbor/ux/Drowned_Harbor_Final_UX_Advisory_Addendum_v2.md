# Drowned Harbor Final UX Advisory Addendum v2

## Authority

This is a bounded metadata addendum to `DH-UX-001`. It does not replace or broaden the governing UX advisory and does not authorize planning or implementation.

## 1. Pixel-independent UX recommendations

The shared display should preserve board-first public-state clarity, stable-seat identity for 1–8 players, and the authority-safe sequence:

`available → focused → selected_preview → confirmation_pending → committed → resolving → settled`

Focus and preview are state-neutral. The first focus never performs an irreversible action. Commitment requires a second explicit action and occurs only after the authoritative owner accepts a legal intent.

Private content remains excluded from captions, transcripts, presentation replay, mirrors, diagnostics, screenshots, and public summaries. Presentation replay never reruns reducers, RNG, events, ending resolution, attribution, or cleanup. Spooky and Grim may change intensity but not geometry, legal actions, focus order, information hierarchy, privacy, mechanisms, or outcomes.

## 2. Clean-room source-planning inputs — gated

Only a separately activated metadata-only planning release may consider stable-seat identity grammar; focus/preview/confirmation/commitment and recovery tokens; neutral private shielding; board occlusion and safe-region rules; public status/caption/prompt/transcript/replay hierarchy; Spooky/Grim intensity layers; and source-to-runtime provenance templates.

These inputs are not permission to draw, model, paint, vectorize, trace, composite, export, or implement.

## 3. Implementation-dependent recommendations — deferred

Final 960×540 layout, drawer and tile dimensions, safe margins, typography, controller focus, glyphs, remapping, private-surface technology, animation timing, captions, audio, haptics, localized copy, fonts, icons, captures, density proof, grayscale/value proof, pseudo-localization, controller/TV/privacy testing, accessibility, and issue #39 evidence remain deferred.

## 4. Unresolved assumptions, questions, and evidence

No successor is active. Source tools and formats, contributors and rights, similarity review, optional provider verification, first-wave planning scope, private-surface technology, occlusion maps, font and localization targets, replay storage, first prototype stage, and interruption/reconnect behavior remain unresolved.

960×540 feasibility, 1/4/8-seat density, safe frame, grayscale/value, text expansion, non-color identity, reduced motion, interruption/restore/replay, private-shield comprehension, privacy/security, television/controller use, accessibility, and issue #39 human evidence remain unproved.

## 5. Corrections and additions to `DH-UX-001`

The former broad rights gate is split into:

1. Owner attestation — complete to the best of firsthand knowledge.
2. R1 private-reference governance — complete with qualifications.
3. Direct generated-pixel/public/commercial clearance — absent and not requested.
4. Clean-room source-planning release — not active.
5. Human-authored source package — absent.
6. Source/runtime-composition authority — absent.

Recommendations are classified as:

- `pixel_independent_advisory`
- `clean_room_source_planning_input_gated`
- `implementation_dependent_deferred`
- `human_evidence_dependent`
- `rejected_or_out_of_scope`

Each named future control must identify authoritative owner, legal-intent source, availability, public/private class, and interaction category. Every measurement remains `hypothesis`, `implemented:false`, `machine_validated:false`, and `human_validated:false` until evidence exists.

## 6. Authority dependencies

`BoardState` owns geometry, spaces, connectors, pawn positions, Tide mutations, and route reachability. `RulesSession` owns legal intents, stage progression, commitments, and public ending result. `RoleSession` owns private roles, objectives, factions, transformations, attribution, and controlled reveal. The session coordinator owns safe handoff, control transfer, rematch, and cleanup. Presentation owns focus, non-mutating preview, animation, emphasis, captions, and presentation-only replay.

Stop when a proposal copies generated pixels, creates source/runtime assets, invents a legal action or runtime field, alters route/Tide authority, exposes private data, transfers coordinator authority, treats storyboards as runtime evidence, claims readability/accessibility/privacy/human success, or activates planning or implementation without a release.

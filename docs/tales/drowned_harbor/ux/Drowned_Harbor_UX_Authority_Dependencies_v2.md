# Drowned Harbor UX Authority Dependencies v2

## Presentation-family dependency

`DH-PRESENT-FAMILY-001` supplies directional continuity across High Water, Last Light, and Ending/Epilogue; presentation-versus-authority boundaries; skip/interruption/replay equivalence; stable-seat public continuity; public/private invariance; and Spooky/Grim information invariance. It does not supply runtime layout, source assets, legal actions, private-surface technology, or human evidence.

## Rights/provenance dependency

`DH-RIGHTS-001` supplies the canonical 25-asset inventory, R1/reference-only/nonproduction classification, provider/file evidence, uncertainty preservation, and no direct-pixel/source/runtime/candidate/public-use authority. Any editable or runtime visual recommendation remains a gated clean-room planning input.

## Owner-attestation dependency

`DH-OWNER-ATTEST-001` supplies completed firsthand account/provider/input/edit-chain statements and establishes eligibility to consider later metadata-only planning. It does not activate planning, source creation, runtime composition, implementation, candidates, public use, or evidence claims.

## Runtime authority dependency

- `BoardState`: geometry, spaces, connectors, pawn positions, Tide mutations, route reachability.
- `RulesSession`: legal intents, stage progression, commitments, public ending result.
- `RoleSession`: private roles, objectives, factions, transformations, attribution, controlled reveal.
- Session coordinator: safe handoff, control transfer, rematch, cleanup.
- Presentation: focus, non-mutating preview, animation, emphasis, captions, presentation-only replay.

No new runtime fields or legal actions are authorized.

## Stop rules

Stop at generated-pixel copying; source/runtime asset creation; invented legal actions or fields; route/Tide authority changes; private-data exposure; coordinator-authority transfer; storyboard promotion to runtime evidence; unproved readability/accessibility/privacy/human claims; or planning/implementation without a separately active release.

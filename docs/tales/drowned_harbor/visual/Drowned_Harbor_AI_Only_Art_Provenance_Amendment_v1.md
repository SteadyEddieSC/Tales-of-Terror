# Drowned Harbor AI-Only Art and Provenance Amendment v1

- **Release:** `AI-ART-POLICY-001`
- **Issue:** #151
- **Amends future production method in:** `DH-SOURCE-PLAN-001`
- **Historical record changed:** no
- **Source art created:** no
- **Runtime art created:** no
- **Implementation authorized:** no

## Purpose

Record the Project Owner's decision that future Drowned Harbor production visuals may use AI-generated or AI-assisted pixels and do not require human-drawn or human-painted source artwork.

This amendment does not rewrite the historical `DH-SOURCE-PLAN-001` registration. It supersedes only specific future-facing requirements.

## Superseded requirements

The following are no longer mandatory for a future separately authorized source release:

- beginning with a blank human-authored editable source;
- treating all direct AI-generated pixel use as permanently prohibited;
- requiring the disposition `pass_independent_human_authorship_supported` as the only path to source acceptance.

A future generated source may instead advance through the `AI-ART-POLICY-001` provenance and promotion lifecycle.

## Preserved protections

The 25 existing external Drowned Harbor images remain:

- `R1_private_internal_reference`;
- `reference_only_nonproduction`;
- not source files;
- ineligible as image-to-image inputs, masks, control images, hidden layers, source textures, alignment overlays, or runtime assets.

The amendment does not allow tracing, vectorization, paint-over, compositing, cropping, upscaling, recoloring, retouching, silhouette extraction, texture extraction, text extraction, logo extraction, or decorative-fragment extraction from those images.

The following remain required:

- BoardState, RulesSession, RoleSession, coordinator, and presentation authority boundaries;
- source-family and control traceability;
- provider, account, model, prompt, input, terms, contributor, tool, font, asset, license, source, transformation, export, and hash records;
- independent similarity review;
- public/private separation;
- one shared Low Tide/High Water board-master direction;
- 960×540 and controller-first presentation constraints;
- Spooky/Grim information invariance;
- issue #39 human evidence;
- no unsupported accessibility, privacy, legal-clearance, production, or shipping claim;
- automation is not human evidence.

## Future AI source method

A future separately activated source release may:

1. use an approved or conditionally eligible provider;
2. generate a new output from text and authorized project-owned inputs;
3. quarantine the original output;
4. record the original SHA-256 and all provider metadata;
5. perform human selection, arrangement, modification, and integration;
6. record human-authored expressive contributions separately;
7. complete rights, similarity, watermark, quality, and continuity review;
8. preserve transformation and export hashes;
9. advance only through explicit promotion states.

The future release must not claim that provider ownership language guarantees copyright protection, exclusivity, non-infringement, or Steam acceptance.

## Current boundary

This amendment authorizes no generation request, source output, editable master, runtime derivative, Godot resource, candidate, ordinary export, marketing image, storefront image, public release, or paid service.

Lantern House remains the sole normal/default Tale. Drowned Harbor remains developer-only and ordinary-export excluded.

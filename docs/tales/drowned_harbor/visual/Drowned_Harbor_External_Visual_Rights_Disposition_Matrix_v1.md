# Drowned Harbor External Visual Rights Disposition Matrix v1

## Tiers

- `R0` — archive only
- `R1` — private internal reference
- `R2` — planning derivative without pixel reuse
- `R3` — human-authored source reconstruction
- `R4` — production candidate
- `R5` — approved production/public asset

## Registered disposition

All 25 external images are fixed at `R1`. Abstract concepts may inform `R2` records. No image, family, or binary is authorized for R3, R4, or R5.

## Permitted

Private critique, comparison, mood and palette reference, silhouette and hierarchy reference, requirements, conversion briefs, and clean-room source-art briefs without pixel reuse.

## Prohibited

Git/public Release storage of binaries; direct game import; source/runtime art; vectorization, tracing, or paint-over preserving generated composition; candidate promotion; final icons, labels, branding or copy; marketing, storefront, merchandise; originality, copyrightability, non-infringement, readability, accessibility, or human-validation claims.

## Future transition

`R1 reference → R2 governed brief → separately authorized R3 clean-room human-authored source → rights/provenance review → R4 candidate process → R5 approval`

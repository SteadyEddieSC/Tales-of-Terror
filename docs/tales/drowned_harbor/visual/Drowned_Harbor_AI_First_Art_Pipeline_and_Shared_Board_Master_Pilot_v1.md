# Drowned Harbor AI-First Art Pipeline and Shared Board-Master Pilot v1

**Release:** `DH-AI-SOURCE-001`
**Issue:** #149
**Registration baseline:** `209bba6498686cd392ddce4bbc32f549d381913f`
**Policy dependency:** `AI-ART-POLICY-001`
**Standing:** metadata-only advisory; no generation or implementation authority

## Purpose

Define the smallest low-budget AI-first experiment capable of testing Drowned Harbor's visual direction without allowing generated pixels to invent gameplay authority or become runtime assets prematurely.

The first bounded target is one invariant board master supporting both Low Tide and High Water. The Tide states must be layers derived from the same geometry, spaces, connectors, anchors, elevation, and safe regions. Separately generated independent boards are prohibited.

## Tool responsibilities

### AI generation

ChatGPT and Gemini may later explore:

- coastline and harbor massing roughs;
- broad terrain and material language;
- mudflat, wet wood, salt-worn stone, marsh, algae, shallow-water, wash, foam, fog, and damp-decay treatments;
- Low Tide and High Water overlay ideas;
- information-preserving Spooky and Grim atmosphere;
- decorative, text-free board ornament concepts.

Generated output is exploratory visual material. It does not own geometry, labels, routes, legal actions, state, information class, or outcomes.

### Ordinary editing

Ordinary non-AI editing owns:

- exact geometry reconstruction;
- alignment and shared-master enforcement;
- layer naming and grouping;
- masks, crops, compositing, cleanup, and rejected-output removal;
- editable-source assembly;
- human selection, arrangement, and documented expressive decisions;
- source and export hashing.

### Godot procedural rendering

A later separately authorized implementation should render exact and changing information rather than bake it into AI images:

- space identifiers and names;
- routes, connectors, reachability, and legal-action emphasis;
- pawn and token positions;
- focus, preview, confirmation, commitment, warning, and recovery states;
- captions, prompts, transcript, replay, public status, and outcomes;
- controller glyphs and localized text;
- private-shield and public-return state;
- accessibility channels and reduced-motion behavior.

### Optional specialist tools

Recraft is optional only for a documented vector or editability gap after visual direction is selected. Firefly is optional only for a specific unmet editing, commercial-positioning, or Content Credentials need. Neither is approved for purchase by this release.

## Planning canvas hypothesis

The external advisory proposes a square editable master at either 4096×4096 or 6144×6144. This remains a planning hypothesis rather than an implementation requirement. The chosen source must support a readable 960×540 shared-screen composition and later crop/export testing without claiming human readability evidence.

## Proposed board-master groups

### `00_METADATA_AND_GUIDES`

Release and source IDs, canvas and color-profile notes, ruler guides, safe-region guides, ownership notes, terms references, source/export hashes, and review state.

### `01_INVARIANT_GEOMETRY_MASTER`

One human-directed exact coastline, harbor-water shape, land masses, structural silhouettes, invariant shoreline anchors, and stable geometry used by every Tide state.

### `02_SPACES_AND_IDS`

Stable space anchors, IDs, footprints, selection zones, and future runtime guide points. Exact names and player-facing labels remain runtime text.

### `03_CONNECTORS_AND_ROUTE_CLASSES`

Connector anchors, endpoints, route classes, and BoardState-owned reachability guides. Generated texture may not determine connectivity.

### `04_TERRAIN_AND_ELEVATION`

Terrain masks, broad material regions, elevation classes, mudflat, wood, stone, marsh, and shallow-water support. These layers may use AI-generated texture studies after generation is separately authorized.

### `05_LOW_TIDE_STATE`

Receded waterline, exposed mudflat, pooled shallows, wet sediment, and tide-revealed surface treatment. It derives from the invariant master and may not alter stable geometry.

### `06_HIGH_WATER_STATE`

Expanded water coverage, inundated low areas, wash, foam, submerged-surface emphasis, and High Water atmosphere. It derives from the same master.

### `07_HAZARD_AND_MUTATION_OVERLAYS`

Public-safe visual support for BoardState- or RulesSession-owned hazards and mutations. Exact hazard state remains data-driven and procedural.

### `08_ANCHORS_AND_RUNTIME_GUIDES`

Pawn/token anchors, camera/crop anchors, future UI attachment points, runtime occlusion guides, and non-export source notes.

### `09_PUBLIC_PRESENTATION_OVERLAYS`

Text-free backing treatments for route emphasis, focus, preview, warnings, recovery, and committed state. Exact status and interaction meaning remains procedural.

### `10_SAFE_REGIONS_AND_TEXT_RESERVES`

Reserved regions for upper status, seat identity, captions, prompts, transcript/replay access, outcome context, and controller-safe margins. These are hypotheses until implementation and issue #39 evidence.

### `11_SPOOKY_GRIM_INTENSITY`

Information-preserving mood layers. Spooky and Grim may vary lighting, fog, decay, texture density, and decorative intensity but must preserve identical geometry, routes, actions, privacy, and outcomes.

### `12_EXPORTS_AND_HASH_NOTES`

Export recipes, target formats, color space, alpha/compression notes, source and export SHA-256 values, and candidate/rejection history.

## Derivation rules

1. Lock authoritative geometry before Tide-state generation.
2. Use the same geometry source for both Tide states.
3. Keep state layers independently toggleable.
4. Keep exact text and dynamic state outside generated images.
5. Keep AI-generated atmosphere subordinate to readability and authority.
6. Record every raw output before editing.
7. Preserve editable assembly and transformation lineage.
8. Reject any output that requires the restricted 25 images as inputs or hidden references.
9. Reject alternate invented spaces, routes, labels, characters, logos, signatures, or private facts.
10. Stop before runtime integration without a separate release.

## Proposed pilot sequence

### Batch 0 — Pre-generation lock

Lock BoardState geometry and authority inputs, selected tools/accounts/privacy modes, prompt/exclusion set, naming, folder proposal, provenance worksheet, terms references, and batch limits. This release does not execute Batch 0.

### Batch 1 — Invariant composition exploration

Six text-only-prompt variants split between ChatGPT and Gemini. Evaluate silhouette, harbor massing, board-safe negative space, and ability to support one shared master. Do not accept generated geometry as authoritative without manual reconstruction.

### Batch 2 — Terrain and material exploration

Four variants for mudflat, wet wood, salt-worn stone, marsh, algae, and shallow-water language.

### Batch 3 — Low Tide overlay exploration

Four variants derived from the locked board silhouette and represented as separate overlays. No invented connectors or labels.

### Batch 4 — High Water overlay exploration

Four variants aligned to the same master and evaluated against the Low Tide state.

### Batch 5 — Spooky and Grim atmosphere exploration

Four variants, two per intensity profile, with identical information and geometry.

### Batch 6 — Optional vector support

Only after a concrete vector gap is documented. Recraft or manual vector work may explore frame accents, backing plates, hazard-ring motifs, or non-text support shapes.

### Batch 7 — Ordinary-editing pilot assembly

One reviewable layered pilot, possibly with one alternate crop or tone pass. It remains a quarantined or assembled planning artifact, not approved production art.

The smallest full pilot is about twenty-two generations before optional vector work. A smaller viability check may use only invariant silhouette, Low Tide, and High Water batches.

## Hard stop conditions

Stop for baked text or labels; invented geometry or routes; state inconsistency; low shared-screen readability; signatures, watermarks, brands, or recognizable protected material; named-artist imitation; private-information risk; color-only meaning; unusable layers; missing provenance; dependence on paint-over; restricted-image use; inability to preserve one shared master; or any request to advance into runtime/public use without a new release.

## Persistent boundary

Lantern House remains the sole normal/default Tale. Drowned Harbor remains developer-only and ordinary-export excluded. No machine or human evidence has established accessibility, television readability, production readiness, or shipping approval.

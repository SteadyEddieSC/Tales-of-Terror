# Drowned Harbor AI Prompt, Tool, and Budget Plan v1

**Release:** `DH-AI-SOURCE-001`
**Standing:** accepted advisory; no generation or purchase authority

## Allocation principles

- Use existing subscriptions before spending money.
- Use AI for exploration, atmosphere, texture, material language, decorative support, and variations.
- Use ordinary editing for exact geometry, alignment, masking, assembly, and editable masters.
- Use Godot for exact text, labels, controller glyphs, routes, interaction state, and changing public information.
- Keep the 25 R1 external images out of every generation tool.
- Do not imitate a named living artist, active studio, franchise, character, logo, trademark, trade dress, celebrity, or private person.
- Defer anything that depends on runtime integration or human evidence.

## General prompt rules

Every prompt family must specify purpose, target output, aspect ratio, source family, editable-format expectation, required composition, excluded information, prohibited references, consistency instructions, variation instructions, acceptance criteria, and likely failure modes.

### Always include

- top-down board-safe composition;
- text-free and label-free output;
- strong silhouette and readable negative space;
- no characters unless separately authorized;
- no gameplay facts beyond the supplied authority brief;
- one shared board-master direction;
- isolated or separable layers where the tool permits;
- original unbranded visual language;
- no private information.

### Always exclude

Readable words, letters, numbers, dates, map labels, route labels, controller glyphs, UI copy, signatures, watermarks, logos, trademarks, branded products, franchise identifiers, real-person likenesses, copyrighted characters, named artists or studios, alternate invented spaces, invented routes, fused or repeated structures, ornamental clutter over safe regions, and color-only critical meaning.

## Prompt family A — Invariant harbor silhouette and massing

**Purpose:** explore top-down coastline, harbor-water shape, land/water breakup, broad structures, and safe negative space before manual exact-geometry reconstruction.

**Target:** square, high-resolution, text-free composition rough. Use no uploaded reference image.

**Base prompt:**

> Create an original top-down board-game environment composition for a fictional drowned coastal harbor. Show a strong readable coastline silhouette, a protected harbor-water shape, broad land masses, a few non-specific structural silhouettes, and generous negative-space regions suitable for later game UI. The image is a composition and material study only, not an authoritative map. Use no text, letters, numbers, labels, icons, routes, tokens, characters, logos, signatures, watermarks, recognizable franchise imagery, or imitation of any named artist or studio. Keep the geometry simple enough for a human to rebuild as one editable master supporting Low Tide and High Water layers.

**Acceptance:** clear silhouette, useful board-safe regions, no authoritative-looking labels/routes, and plausible manual reconstruction.

**Reject:** decorative illustration that cannot become a board, invented route network, baked labels, recognizable IP, or excessive perspective.

## Prompt family B — Terrain and material sheet

**Purpose:** explore separable material language for mudflat, wet wood, salt-worn stone, marsh edge, algae stain, shallow water, wash, and foam.

**Base prompt:**

> Create an original top-down material and surface study for a fictional drowned-harbor board game. Present separate, clean, non-overlapping samples of exposed tidal mud, wet aged wood, salt-worn stone, marsh-edge vegetation, algae staining, shallow-water sheen, white-water wash, and restrained foam. No map, text, labels, icons, characters, logos, brands, signatures, watermarks, or named-artist imitation. Keep the samples tileable or mask-friendly and readable at small shared-screen scale.

**Acceptance:** separable surfaces, coherent palette, no text, and useful masking potential.

## Prompt family C — Low Tide overlay

**Purpose:** explore receded waterline, exposed mudflat, pooled shallows, wet sediment, and tide-revealed surfaces without changing geometry.

**Base prompt:**

> Create a text-free transparent-style Low Tide environmental overlay for an existing fictional harbor-board master. The invariant coastline, spaces, connectors, and structures are already fixed and must not be redesigned. Show only receded water coverage, exposed mudflat, pooled shallows, wet sediment, tide marks, and subtle revealed-surface mood. No new islands, buildings, routes, labels, icons, characters, text, logos, signatures, or watermarks. The result must align to one shared master and remain separable from the base geometry.

**Acceptance:** reads as a state layer rather than a new board.

## Prompt family D — High Water overlay

**Purpose:** explore expanded water coverage, inundation, wash, foam, and submerged-surface emphasis using the same geometry.

**Base prompt:**

> Create a text-free transparent-style High Water environmental overlay for the same existing fictional harbor-board master. Do not redesign the coastline, spaces, connectors, structures, or route geometry. Show expanded water coverage, inundated low areas, restrained wash and foam, submerged-surface emphasis, and readable water movement. No new geography, routes, labels, icons, characters, text, logos, signatures, or watermarks. The result must align exactly with the shared master and remain separable from the base geometry.

**Acceptance:** visibly different from Low Tide but geometrically identical.

## Prompt family E — Spooky and Grim intensity layers

**Purpose:** create two mood treatments that preserve identical information.

**Spooky direction:** eerie fog, lighter uncanny shadows, restrained luminescence, readable tension.

**Grim direction:** colder light, heavier damp decay, denser shadow and texture, but no loss of board readability.

**Base prompt:**

> Create a separable, text-free atmosphere layer for an original top-down drowned-harbor board. Preserve all underlying geometry, routes, state, safe regions, and information. Add only mood through fog, lighting, shadow, dampness, and restrained environmental texture. Do not add objects, characters, labels, icons, routes, logos, signatures, watermarks, or story facts. Maintain readable contrast and do not rely on color alone for any meaning.

**Acceptance:** mood changes, information does not.

## Prompt family F — Vector-friendly ornaments and support motifs

This family is deferred until a vector gap exists. It may later request simple original frame accents, backing plates, hazard-ring motifs, or non-text overlay shapes. It must exclude letters, numbers, controller glyphs, legal-action symbols, logos, brands, and gameplay-authority icons.

## Variation policy

Change only one or two controlled variables per variant—such as coastline massing, texture density, fog amount, or wash intensity. Do not prompt tools to “surprise” the project with new gameplay content. Preserve prompt revisions and reject reasons.

## Budget decision

- Immediate incremental cost: `$0`.
- Initial complete pilot: about 22 generations using existing ChatGPT and Gemini subscriptions.
- Optional earliest specialist test: `$1` non-sensitive Recraft API prompt-only experiment after a vector gap is named.
- Private Recraft month: only after visual direction is selected and manual cleanup is insufficient.
- Firefly month: only after a remaining documented gap.
- Multiple simultaneous subscriptions: prohibited without a later decision.
- Do not purchase Midjourney, Leonardo, local AI hardware, LoRAs, checkpoints, third-party model packs, asset packs, paid fonts, plugins, contractor services, or artist work through this release.

## Purpose

Convert the governed Drowned Harbor preproduction corpus into a controller-first, shared-screen interaction storyboard package that makes public information, private shielding, legal actions, focus order, confirmation, persistent text, stable-seat continuity, captions, transcript behavior, state variants, and human-review questions explicit.

The package is intended to be implementation-ready as a design input while remaining mechanically and legally unable to become a production Tale by itself.

## Closed storyboard record contract

Every record declares:

- stable ID and title;
- category and stage context;
- layout and privacy surface;
- entry and exit conditions;
- purpose and required information;
- named layout regions;
- stable legal actions;
- confirmation pattern;
- focus order;
- captions and transcript rules;
- persistent-text policy;
- stable-seat authority policy;
- state variants;
- visual guidance and negative constraints;
- source paths and cross-media concepts;
- human-validation questions;
- preproduction status and approval boundary.

Unknown fields reject.

## Public and private boundary

The television may show only authoritative public information. Private bargain terms and inherited private state require:

1. a safe handoff;
2. a neutral public shield;
3. an authorized controlled-private surface;
4. explicit acknowledgement;
5. private-content clearing before shared play resumes.

Private content may not enter public audio, captions, transcript, replay, mirrored presentation, seat summaries, or public history.

## Stable-seat boundary

Disconnect, game control, takeover, return, transformation, defeat, Restless continuation, and ending attribution retain the same stable-seat identity and evolved state.

The storyboard pack prohibits control-change language or presentation that implies:

- a new character;
- respawn;
- healing;
- reroll;
- restored inventory;
- reset objectives;
- eliminated-player spectating;
- loss of votes, actions, takeover eligibility, or ending attribution.

## High Water boundary

High Water is represented by two required records:

- `DH-UI-008`: committed before-state, deterministic transformation, changed-category summary, and skip-equivalent presentation;
- `DH-UI-009`: transformed playable board, route types, public forms, rescue/hazard windows, and legal actions.

Presentation may not change the result. Skipping animation must lead to the same authoritative state and summary.

## Accessibility and localization boundary

Critical state, choices, consequences, ownership, and public outcomes remain available through persistent text and plain-system wording when voice, music, SFX, animation, color, or English word order is unavailable.

The package carries provisional two-line and 42-character-per-line caption targets. These are internal design constraints, not compliance or human-readability evidence.

## Offline renderer

The standard-library renderer creates a deterministic standalone HTML review file with all 22 storyboard IDs and no network, JavaScript, external stylesheet, remote image, remote font, or tracking dependency.

The output is a wireframe review aid. It is not a screenshot of implemented UI and is not production evidence.

## Automated checks

The package validator checks:

- exact fields and design-only status;
- 22-screen inventory and unique identity;
- category coverage;
- known stage, layout, privacy, action, confirmation, and status values;
- source resolution;
- known traceability concepts;
- private-shield and public-history restrictions;
- caption requirements;
- persistent critical text;
- stable-seat continuity;
- controlled authority transfer;
- High Water transition/board pairing.

The mutation suite includes eighteen fail-closed cases covering:

- duplicate or missing records;
- production-status drift;
- unknown runtime fields;
- missing source files;
- unknown traceability IDs;
- broken private shielding;
- private transcript leakage;
- missing confirmation;
- lost persistent text;
- lost stable-seat identity or control-source display;
- broken High Water pairing;
- prohibited or missing authority transfer;
- caption expansion drift;
- missing human-review questions;
- incomplete category coverage.

Renderer tests check deterministic ordering, all IDs and links, expected shared-screen regions, privacy markers, offline output, and absence of scripts or external sources.

## Human validation still blocked

No claim is made about:

- physical-controller behavior;
- focus comprehension;
- television readability;
- caption speed or enlargement;
- private-shield leakage on real hardware;
- screen-reader or assistive-technology behavior;
- animation clarity;
- voice or sound masking;
- household pacing, tension, fun, fairness, or balance.

These require exact-build human review after implementation is separately authorized.

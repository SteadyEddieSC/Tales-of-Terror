## Registered result

The bounded target is one shared Low Tide / High Water board master.

The advisory defines eight batches.

This release registers a bounded AI-first production advisory for one future Drowned Harbor shared Low Tide / High Water board-master pilot. It records:

- ChatGPT and Gemini as the existing-subscription concept and atmosphere tools;
- ordinary non-AI editing as the exact-geometry and pilot-assembly authority;
- Godot procedural rendering as the future authority for exact text, labels, route emphasis, focus, preview, warning, recovery, commitment, captions, prompts, and changing state;
- optional Recraft only after a concrete editable-vector gap is demonstrated;
- optional Adobe Firefly only after a separate documented capability or Content Credentials need;
- one invariant board geometry from which Low Tide and High Water layers derive;
- thirteen proposed board-master groups;
- six prompt families with exclusions and failure criteria;
- an eight-batch sequence with about twenty-two initial generations as the smallest full pilot;
- stable naming, future folder, provenance, hash, review, and rejection conventions;
- immediate `$0` incremental spend;
- a Release Coordinator supplement allowing an optional `$1` non-sensitive Recraft API experiment before a private paid month;
- six immediate generation candidates, still ungenerated;
- unresolved source dimensions, runtime interfaces, pricing, reviewer details, Steam wording, and human-evidence dependencies.

## Release Coordinator supplements

The accepted advisory is governed by these additional controls:

1. Before project generation, use ChatGPT Temporary Chat or disable `Improve the model for everyone`.
2. Before project generation, use Gemini Temporary Chat or disable Keep Activity; do not submit feedback on proprietary generation conversations.
3. Do not use Recraft Free outputs in the game.
4. A `$1` Recraft API test may be used only for non-sensitive prompt-only experimentation because temporary result links may be accessible to anyone who knows the signed URL.
5. Use a private paid Recraft plan only after a specific vector/editability gap is documented.
6. Firefly remains a fallback rather than an approved purchase now.
7. No existing R1 image or unlicensed third-party input may be uploaded to any tool.
8. Exact provider eligibility and later asset promotion remain governed by `AI-ART-POLICY-001`.

## Authority and information boundary

- `BoardState` owns board geometry, spaces, connectors, routes, pawn positions, Tide mutations, and reachability.
- `RulesSession` owns legal intents, commitments, public warnings, recovery projections, stage progression, and public outcomes.
- `RoleSession` owns private roles, objectives, factions, transformations, attribution, and controlled reveals.
- The session coordinator owns reconnect, surrogate control, takeover-pending state, safe handoff, public return, rematch, and cleanup.
- Presentation owns focus, non-mutating preview, animation, emphasis, captions, and presentation-only replay.

AI output may support mood, materials, texture, atmosphere, ornament, and composition exploration. It may not invent authoritative geometry, routes, legal actions, private information, labels, state, or outcomes.

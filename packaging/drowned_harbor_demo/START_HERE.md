# Drowned Harbor — Alpha.4 demo

Launch `drowned_harbor_demo.exe` on Windows, or make `drowned_harbor_demo.x86_64`
executable and launch it on Linux. This is a developer demo of Terror Turn's
second Tale. It does not require Godot, a companion phone, or an Internet connection.

Press A or Enter to begin. Each controller presses A in the lobby to join.
Keyboard development fallback: J adds a pass-and-play seat; Tab changes keyboard
seat. The active keyboard seat also follows the turn. Up/down, D-pad or stick
chooses; A/Enter commits; B/Escape goes back; Y/V opens your private hand;
Start/P pauses; X/H opens help. The primary route requires no mouse.

Cooperative supports 1–8 seats, Hidden Betrayer 3–8, and Outbreak 2–8.
Unsupported social counts use the cooperative fallback. Everyone except the
named seat must look away during a private handoff; the television is a shared
surface, not a confidential personal display.

Gather supplies and evidence before High Water. Rescue residents, manage the
Harbor's claim, and vote on the final lighthouse commitment. Resources and choices
determine the ending. Defeated witnesses remain participants. Pause to inspect
the crew inventory, save, adjust presentation or reclaim a disconnected seat.
Inventory pages separate supplies, cards and threats. The Witnesses page lists
public forms and explicitly declared allegiances. Other secrets stay shielded.

Continue reads the last valid local save or its backup. Saves belong to the
native demo and include private game information; do not share them as public
playtest reports. Other prototype save versions are rejected clearly.
When a restored seat's turn arrives, its controller can press A to reclaim it.
Pause also offers keyboard reclaim. Read both private pages before continuing.

Settings include three narrative profiles, reduced motion, large text, captions,
and separate master/music/effects levels. Current speech is captioned text;
original synthesized ambience, music beds and cues are included. The seven
illustrations were generated with OpenAI image generation without external image
references. Independent human art, accessibility and controller review remains pending.

Known demo limits: the production catalog still contains Lantern House only;
phones do not control this new demo; no online multiplayer; six landmarks rather
than the full planned town; physical 1–8-controller and living-room testing remain
necessary. Hunted, campaign play and recorded Underteller speech are not included.
